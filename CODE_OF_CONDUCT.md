# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the spirit of **Akwaaba** (the Ghanaian tradition of heartfelt hospitality, dignity, and mutual respect), we as members, contributors, and maintainers of **Akwaaba POS & Retail OS** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward other people.
* **Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and retail business realities** (e.g., understanding the unique constraints of merchants operating with Dumsor blackouts, intermittent cellular data, and inflation).
* **Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback**.
* **Accepting responsibility and apologizing** to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
* **Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals**, but for the overall merchant community and ecosystem.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainer **Mazonia** is responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including the project's GitHub repository, issue trackers, discussions, chat channels, and any offline or online events where an individual is representing the project.

---

## Reporting Guidelines & Contact

Because there are currently no developer email addresses assigned for this project, instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported directly to maintainer **Mazonia** through the repository issue tracker:

👉 **[Submit Complaint on GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Mazonia/pos-n-sales-system/issues)**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly with confidentiality.
